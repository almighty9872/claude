The Holy Rosary

The Rosary is a way of meditating deeply, together with Mary, on the mysteries of the life of Jesus Christ. Below you will find the prayers in English and Turkish, the mysteries, and how to pray the Rosary. The mystery for today is marked automatically.

Pray the Rosary, Bead by Bead

Tap the glowing bead or press Next; the prayer for each bead appears in the prayer panel. Today's mysteries are chosen for you.

How to Pray the Rosary

  1. Begin with the Sign of the Cross and say the Apostles’ Creed on the crucifix.
  2. On the first large bead, say the Our Father.
  3. On the next three small beads, say three Hail Marys for faith, hope and love.
  4. Say the Glory Be.
  5. Announce the first mystery of the day and say the Our Father on the large bead.
  6. On the ten small beads, say ten Hail Marys while meditating on the mystery.
  7. Say the Glory Be, then the Fatima Prayer.
  8. Repeat steps 5, 6 and 7 until all five mysteries are complete.
  9. Conclude with the Hail Holy Queen and the closing prayer, then the Sign of the Cross.

The prayer texts follow the Turkish devotional tradition used for the Rosary at St. Anthony of Padua Basilica in Istanbul; this English page shows the original English wording of each prayer as given alongside it.
