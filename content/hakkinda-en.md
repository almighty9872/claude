## Texts

- **Catechism:** the [Compendium of the Catechism of the Catholic Church](https://www.vatican.va/archive/compendium_ccc/documents/archive_2005_compendium-ccc_en.html). The text is © 2005 Libreria Editrice Vaticana; the English is the Vatican’s own.
- **Scripture:** the English of the verses in the popups is the public-domain Douay-Rheims translation. The link in the popup opens the passage on BibleGateway (RSVCE).
- **The Mass:** excerpts from the English translation of *The Roman Missal* © 2010, International Commission on English in the Liturgy Corporation. All rights reserved.
- **The Rosary:** the traditional English prayers, in the public domain.
- **Saints:** dates follow the General Roman Calendar and the Roman Martyrology.

## Written for this site

Everything else (the lives of the saints, the parables, the miracles, the history of the Church, the guides, Answering Islam, Answering Atheism, A Call to Our Jewish Friends) was written for this site from the Church’s documents and reliable sources. Where a page draws on particular sources, they are listed at the end of that page.

- **Answering Islam:** hadith numbers follow sunnah.com. The Arabic of the Qur’an comes from the Tanzil project, the English from Pickthall’s translation (1930).
- **Miracles:** the sections on Fatima, Lanciano and Padre Pio draw on Ethan Muse’s [Motiva Credibilitatis](https://motivacredibilitatis.substack.com/).
